Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find answers to common questions about our loan application process, fees, eligibility, and more.

1

What is LoanMitra AI?

LoanMitra AI is an AI-powered loan assistance platform that helps applicants check eligibility, complete KYC, upload documents, and track their loan application through a transparent digital dashboard.

2

How does the loan application process work?

  1. Register your account.
  2. Complete KYC verification.
  3. Upload required documents.
  4. AI profile analysis.
  5. Lender matching.
  6. Loan review.
  7. Approval or rejection.
  8. Fund disbursement (if approved).
3

What types of loans are available?

  • • Personal Loan
  • • Business Loan
  • • Home Loan
  • • Vehicle Loan
  • • MSME Loan
  • • Loan Against Property
  • • Working Capital Loan
  • • Professional Loan
4

Is there any processing fee?

Yes.

The processing fee is 1% of the requested loan amount.

5

Is the processing fee refundable?

If your application is approved by any participating lender, the processing fee becomes non-refundable.

If all available lender attempts are unsuccessful, the refund will be processed according to our Refund Policy.

6

How many lenders will review my application?

Your application may be reviewed by up to 3 lenders, depending on your eligibility and the review process.

7

Can I apply with a low CIBIL score?

Yes.

Applications with low CIBIL scores are welcome and may still be considered, subject to eligibility and lender review.

8

Is loan approval guaranteed?

No.

Loan approval depends on lender eligibility criteria, document verification, credit assessment, and final review.

9

How can I track my application?

Simply log in to your dashboard using your Application ID and registered mobile number to view real-time application updates.

10

How long does the application process take?

Processing time varies depending on document verification, applicant profile, and lender review.

11

What documents are required?

Generally required documents include:

  • • Aadhaar Card
  • • PAN Card
  • • Bank Statement
  • • Salary Slip (if applicable)
  • • ITR (if applicable)
  • • GST Documents (for businesses)
  • • Property Documents (if applicable)
12

Is my personal information secure?

Yes.

Your information and documents are protected through secure digital systems and encrypted storage.

13

Can I update my documents after registration?

Yes.

You can upload or update requested documents through your dashboard whenever required.

14

How will I receive loan updates?

All important updates are available in your dashboard.

You may also receive notifications through SMS, email, or WhatsApp.

15

How can I contact support?

You can contact our support team through:

  • Phone Support
  • Email Support
  • WhatsApp Support
  • Support Ticket from Dashboard
16

Can I cancel my application?

Yes.

Applications may be cancelled before final processing, subject to our Terms & Conditions.

17

Why should I choose LoanMitra AI?

  • AI-Powered Analysis
  • Real-Time Dashboard
  • Digital KYC Process
  • Secure Document Handling
  • Transparent Application Tracking
  • Dedicated Customer Support
